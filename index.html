About HMS

Simplifying Hostel Operations

Hostel Management System is designed to bring efficiency, clarity, and transparency to college residential management.

The system replaces manual registers and spreadsheets with an intuitive dashboard for room allocation, student records, staff details, fee tracking, and complaint handling.

Mission and Objectives

  • Accuracy: Reduce duplicate profiles and incorrect room reports.
  • Transparency: Keep fee receipts and complaint progress easy to track.
  • Security: Use role-based access for students, staff, and admins.
