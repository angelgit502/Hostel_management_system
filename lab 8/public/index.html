Hostel Management System

A digital room allocation & management platform for campus hostels, powered by Express.js, Mongoose & MongoDB.

About the System

System Overview

The Hostel Management System is a web-based administrative platform developed to simplify campus hostel operations. It replaces manual registers with a digital room management workflow, allowing staff to track room status, enforce capacity limits, and maintain room records efficiently.

Architecture & Purpose

Built as a RESTful service implementation, the system separates frontend interaction from backend business logic. Fetch API communicates seamlessly with Express REST endpoints, persisting room states reliably inside a MongoDB database.
